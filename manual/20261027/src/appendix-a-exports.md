### CDBCHECKBOUNDDTL detail

- Caches horizontal/vertical twips-per-pixel from `GetDeviceCaps` (indices 0x58 / 0x5a).
- `param_1 == 'X'` checks against horizontal limit; `'Y'` against vertical.
- Returns 1 if the value is within range, 0 otherwise.
- Called at four sites inside `CDBSENDCREATE` and two inside `CDBNEWOBJECT`.
- This is the clamp, not the disk-to-object bounds reader.

| `CDBSKIPSEP` | 55 | 95 (12f0) | 04b4 | Separator skipper. Called by `CDBCHUNKFIND`, `CDBCHUNKLAST`, `CDBCHUNKREMOVE`. Modes: 1 = nonempty check, 2 = CRLF pair, 3 = whitespace. |

## Where Foolbook 1.0.5r32 disagrees

The live class `0x10` record is 53 bytes and ends at `+0x34`. The count is the word at `+0x30`. The points are the block named by the word at `+0x32`, not an inline list. The source rectangle is `+0x28` through `+0x2F`. Paint maps a point by `x' = (x − src.left) × dstW / srcW + dst.left`, and the same for y. P430 object 136 has 11 points and the scale is 1:1.

Foolbook 1.0.5r32 reads an inline list at body `+0x3A` and treats the bytes at body `+0x26` as not a source rectangle.

The field named `correct` is not hidden. A click sets its fill to 255,255,63. `enterPage` sets that fill back to 64,255,255. Foolbook 1.0.5r32 leaves the field hidden.
